Can quantum spin squeezing in NMR systems help the search for new particles?

We consider an ensemble of $$N$$ identical two-level systems (spins) coupled to a single-mode cavity field. The system is described by the Tavis-Cummings Hamiltonian (assuming the rotating-wave approximation and $$\hbar = 1$$):
\begin{equation}
    H = \omega_c a^\dagger a + \omega_s J_z + g \left( a^\dagger J_- + a J_+ \right),
\end{equation}
where $$\omega_c$$ is the cavity mode frequency, $$\omega_s$$ the spin transition frequency, $$a$$ $$(a^\dagger)$$ is the annihilation (creation) operator for the cavity mode, and $$J_\pm = \sum_{i=1}^N \sigma_\pm^{(i)}$$ are collective spin raising and lowering operators. The collective operators obey angular momentum algebra with total spin $$J = N/2$$. In the dispersive regime, the detuning
\begin{equation}
    \Delta = \omega_c - \omega_s
\end{equation}
is large compared to the collective coupling, i.e., $$|\Delta| \gg g \sqrt{N}$$. In this limit, real energy exchange between the spins and the cavity is strongly suppressed, but virtual photon exchange leads to effective spin–spin interactions. The effective Hamiltonian contains a non-linear term, the so-called one-axis twisting (OAT) Hamiltonian, first proposed by Kitagawa and Ueda, which is known to generate spin-squeezed states.

#### Standard squeezing protocol in the $$yz$$-plane

The conventional OAT squeezing protocol, used in most cavity, circuit, and atomic experiments (e.g. left panel setup schematic). [^1]

By initialising all spins along $$x$$ (equatorial coherent spin state, CSS) with a $$\pi/2$$ pulse, the system than evolves under the OAT Hamiltonian
\begin{equation}
    H_{\text{OAT}} = \hbar \chi J_z^2.
\end{equation}

For short times $$\mu = \chi t N \ll 1$$, the squeezing parameter scales as $$\xi^2 \propto N^{-2/3}$$,
with the squeezed quadrature rotating in the $$yz$$-plane as $$\theta \approx (N \chi t)^{-1}$$.
This protocol produces squeezing perpendicular to the mean spin in the $$yz$$-plane



#### Equatorial squeezing protocol in the $$xy$$-plane 


A protocol that generates OAT squeezing directly in the equatorial $$xy$$-plane while keeping the mean spin along the $$z$$-direction, and avoiding transverse decoherence that plagues standard protocols in nuclear-spin systems has been recently proposed (e.g. right panel setup schematic). [^2]

 In the dispersive, far-detuned regime, one finds the effective Hamiltonian
\begin{equation}
    H^x_{\text{OAT}} = \hbar\chi J_x^2.  
\end{equation}
Evolution under $$H^x_{\text{OAT}}$$ directly squeezes fluctuations in the $$xy$$-plane (rotating frame) while the mean spin remains along $$z$$. 





#### Dissipation and new physics

Under collective (coherent) effects of cosmic neutrinos or axion-like dark matter, the variance grows over time, producing an anti-squeezing effect. This behavior could leave a detectable imprint on the system, provided that all other dissipative channels are sufficiently suppressed. These include $$T_1$$ and $$T_2$$ relaxation, collective decays (radiation damping) arising from circuit back-action and finite polarisation.
